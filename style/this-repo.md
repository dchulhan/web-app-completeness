# House style for this repository

- Second person. Active voice. Sentence-case headings.
- Short paragraphs. Serial commas.
- Conditions before instructions.
- Descriptive link text.
- Queen's English in prose. Vendor names stay as the vendor spells them.
- Unambiguous dates: 27 September 2026.
- Every new rule needs id, severity, check, and fix.
- No pre-announcements. No simply or just. No jokes in RULES.yaml.
